FEATURES

Attendance that takes itself.

Attendly replaces paper registers and roll calls with a fast, accurate and effortless experience for teachers and students.

Face ID login

Students sign in with a quick photo. No usernames, no passwords to forget, and new students can register in under a minute.

AI photo attendance

Take or upload one or more photos of the class. Everyone Attendly recognises is marked present automatically, even in a group shot.

Voice attendance

Prefer a roll call? Record a short clip and Attendly identifies who spoke. A great backup when cameras aren't an option.

Review before you save

See exactly who was detected and who wasn't before confirming. You stay in control of every attendance record.

Join with a code or QR

Create a subject and share its link or QR code. Students join their class in a single tap, straight from WhatsApp or the classroom screen.

Instant reports

Per-student attendance percentage, classes attended and missed, plus a full history of every class you've taken.

Your students' dashboard

Students see their subjects and live attendance percentage at a glance, so everyone knows where they stand.

Works on any device

Phone, tablet or laptop. Nothing to install and nothing to set up. Just open it in a browser and go.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need any special hardware?

No. Any phone, tablet or laptop with a camera works. Voice attendance only needs a microphone.

What if a student isn't recognised?

You review the results before saving, so you can see who was detected and who wasn't. For the best results, students can add a few extra face photos from their dashboard, and class photos taken in good light with faces clearly visible work best.

How do students join a class?

Teachers share a class link or QR code. Students open it, sign in with Face ID and are enrolled right away.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Attendly runs in your browser on any modern phone or computer. There's nothing to download.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Open the app and get started.
